Our story

About East West Institute of Technology

An autonomous engineering institution in west Bengaluru, shaped by two decades of teaching, research and placement outcomes — and by the 57-year heritage of the East West Group of Institutions.

Who we are

A campus built for engineering practice

East West Institute of Technology was established in 2001 on a self-contained 20-acre campus in Anjananagar, off Magadi Main Road — a location well connected to Rajajinagar, Vijayanagar and Kengeri by the city's bus network.

The institute is recognised under sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act, approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and recognised by the Government of Karnataka. It is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi, and holds UGC autonomy, which lets departments design their own electives and assessments on top of the VTU framework.

EWIT offers eleven B.E. disciplines alongside MBA, MCA and five M.Tech streams — eighteen programmes in all — supported by laboratories across the engineering branches, a central library of more than 65,000 volumes, and a dedicated research centre.

Heritage

From a 1968 founding to an autonomous institute

The East West Group of Institutions began its educational work in 1968. EWIT is the group's flagship engineering campus.

1968

The East West Group is founded

Beginning a 57-year legacy of education in Karnataka that today spans engineering, polytechnic and management institutions.

2001

EWIT is established

East West Institute of Technology opens on the Magadi Road campus in Vishwaneedam, Anjananagar — a self-contained 20-acre site in west Bengaluru.

2000s–2010s

Programmes and accreditation grow

The institute expands across engineering disciplines and adds management and postgraduate programmes, alongside AICTE approval and NBA accreditation.

Today

Autonomy, NAAC 'A' and dual accreditation

EWIT holds UGC autonomy under VTU, NAAC Grade 'A' with a CGPA of 3.25 (valid to October 2028), NBA accreditation, and a portfolio of eighteen UG and PG programmes.

Vision

Engineers who lead

To be a centre of technical education that produces competent, ethical and industry-ready engineers and managers, contributing to the region and the nation.

Mission

Teaching, research, outcomes

To deliver rigorous, continuously refreshed curricula; to support faculty research and industry collaboration; and to prepare every student for meaningful careers and higher study.
