Library & resources

A central collection of more than 65,000 volumes, thousands of titles, print journals and VTU consortium e-journal access.

Central library

A library built for engineering study

The central library serves every department on campus, with a collection of more than 65,000 volumes, thousands of titles, and a wide set of national and international print journals.

Students and faculty also have access to the VTU consortium of e-journals, giving the campus digital access to major scientific publishers, alongside departmental reading collections and dedicated reading halls.

  • 65,000+ volumes across 10,800+ titles
  • 85 national and international printed journals
  • VTU consortium e-journals across major publishers
  • Departmental libraries and digital resources
