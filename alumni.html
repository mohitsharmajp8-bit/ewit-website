Alumni

Two decades of graduates

Since the institute opened in 2001, EWIT graduates have gone into software and product engineering, core engineering, banking and analytics, higher study and entrepreneurship - many of them staying in Bengaluru's technology and engineering ecosystem.

Our alumni return as mentors, guest speakers and recruiters, and help current students through referrals and industry exposure. A good proportion of students also go on to higher studies abroad, civil services and founding their own ventures.

  • Mentoring and guest lectures for current students
  • Referrals and off-campus opportunities
  • Part of the wider East West Group network since 1968
