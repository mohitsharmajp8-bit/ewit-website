How we prepare students

Readiness, not just eligibility

The Training & Placement Cell runs through the academic year with 24 structured modules spanning aptitude, group discussion, communication and core technical skills — reinforced by mock drives, industry seminars and live projects from the first year.

  • 24 structured aptitude, GD and communication modules
  • Mock drives, mock interviews and industry seminars
  • Active relationships with 300+ companies across sectors
  • Exclusive EWIT drives plus joint East West Group pool drives
  • Off-campus drive notifications, walk-ins and referrals shared with registered students
The season

How the placement cycle runs

Semester 7 · July–August

Registration & pre-placement talks

Students register with the cell; company criteria and cut-offs are communicated ahead of each drive.

October onwards

Company drives begin

Exclusive EWIT drives and joint East West Group pool drives run on and off campus through the season.

Through April

Interviews & offers

Offer letters are issued on a rolling basis throughout the season as students clear company processes.

Eligibility

What recruiters expect

No active backlogs at registration, and typically 60%+ aggregate across semesters — individual company criteria may vary.
