Admissions 2026–27

Admissions & eligibility

Three routes to a seat at EWIT — KCET counselling, COMEDK UGET, or the management quota. Here is what you need to know and how to apply.

Routes to admission

Choose the path that fits you

Government quota

KCET Counselling

Apply through Karnataka Examinations Authority counselling using EWIT code E106. Seats are allotted by KEA based on CET rank and reservation norms.

Private universities' quota

COMEDK UGET

Apply through COMEDK UGET counselling using EWIT code E048 for seats allotted on the basis of the COMEDK rank.

Direct admission

Management Quota

Open to candidates who have completed 10+2 / PUC with Physics and Mathematics and at least 45% aggregate — no KCET or COMEDK score required.

The process

From enquiry to enrolment

Enquire

Share your details with the admissions office by phone, email or the form on this page.

Check eligibility

Confirm your qualifying examination, aggregate marks and the admission route that applies to you.

Apply & counsel

Participate in KCET or COMEDK counselling, or complete the management-quota application directly.

Document verification

Submit original documents for verification and complete the admission formalities on campus.

Document checklist

Keep these ready

Carry originals and two sets of photocopies for verification. The admissions office will confirm any additional documents specific to your route.

  • 10+2 / PUC marks card and pass certificate
  • KCET / COMEDK rank card (where applicable)
  • SSLC / 10th marks card for date of birth
  • Transfer certificate and conduct certificate
  • Caste / income certificate (for applicable categories)
  • Passport-size photographs and ID proof
