About the institute

Two decades of engineering education on Magadi Road

Established in 2001, East West Institute of Technology sits on a self-contained 20-acre campus in Anjananagar, west Bengaluru, and traces its educational heritage to the East West Group's founding in 1968 — a legacy of 57 years.

Today EWIT is a UGC-autonomous institution affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi, with dual NAAC and NBA accreditation and a portfolio of eleven B.E. disciplines alongside MBA, MCA and five M.Tech streams.

  • Recognised under UGC Act 2(f) and 12(B)
  • Approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and Govt. of Karnataka
  • Central library of 65,000+ volumes and VTU consortium e-journals
  • Separate in-campus hostels, sports grounds and a health centre
