Campus life

Everything a student needs, on one campus

EWIT sits on a self-contained 20-acre campus in Anjananagar, west Bengaluru — laboratories, a central library, separate hostels, sports grounds and daily amenities, all secured in one place.

Facilities

Built for study and for life

Central Library

65,000+ volumes

A well-stocked central library with thousands of titles, national and international journals, and VTU consortium e-journal access to IEEE, Springer, Elsevier ScienceDirect, Taylor & Francis and ASCE.

Laboratories

Labs across ten branches

Well-equipped laboratories supporting teaching and research across the engineering branches, including more than 215 high-end systems in computing and a GPU-backed AI / ML lab.

Hostels

Separate boys' & girls' hostels

In-campus, hygienic accommodation managed by a resident warden, with menus planned as a mix of North and South Indian food prepared in the hostel kitchen.

Sports

Nine disciplines

Outdoor grounds and courts alongside an indoor games suite and a campus gymnasium — cricket, football, volleyball, badminton, handball and more.

Dining

Cafeteria & canteen

A cafeteria and hygienic canteen serving daily meals for resident and day scholars alike.

Amenities

One secured campus

Campus-wide Wi-Fi, a college transport fleet, an on-campus bank and ATM, a health and medical centre, photocopy centre and parking.

Residential life

A hostel that feels like a second home

EWIT provides comfortable, well-maintained in-campus accommodation separately for boys and girls. A resident warden manages the hostels and handles day-to-day matters, supported by technically trained staff for maintenance.

With students arriving from across the country, the menu is planned as a mix of North Indian and South Indian food with variety each day — with a focus on hygiene, quality and cleanliness in the hostel kitchen.

  • Separate hostels for boys and girls, within the campus
  • Warden-managed, with student involvement in daily running
  • Hygienic kitchen with a varied North and South Indian menu
