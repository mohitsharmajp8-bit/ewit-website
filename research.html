Research & Innovation

Funded projects, faculty publications and industry collaborations across AI, cybersecurity, IoT, electronics and core engineering.

Research & innovation

Research that runs alongside teaching

Faculty across departments publish in national and international journals and conferences, hold funded projects, and bring that work into the classroom. As a UGC-autonomous institution under VTU, EWIT can shape its own electives and assessment around emerging fields.

Research activity is concentrated in applied computing, AI and machine learning, cybersecurity and blockchain, communication systems, and civil and mechanical engineering.

  • 6 sanctioned funded projects in the CSE department alone
  • 13 active industry MoUs supporting projects and internships
  • Faculty publications with DOI-indexed journals and conference papers
Research areas

Where our faculty work

AI & machine learning

Applied ML, deep learning, computer vision and predictive modelling, including health-informatics applications.

Cybersecurity & blockchain

Cryptographic protocols, data-integrity verification, IoT security and distributed-ledger applications.

Data science & IoT

Sensor networks, real-time monitoring, edge intelligence and analytics for engineering systems.

Electronics & VLSI

Circuit design, embedded systems and communication engineering with laboratory modernisation support.

Structures & geotechnics

Structural analysis and design, ground engineering and construction materials.

Machine design & thermal

Design optimisation, simulation and thermal engineering for core-industry problems.
