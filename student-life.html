Campus Life

Student life

Sports, clubs, cultural activity and residential life on a self-contained campus in west Bengaluru.

Student life

More than lectures

Technical clubs, cultural activity, sport and residential life - all on the same 20-acre campus in Anjananagar.

Sports & recreation

Cricket ground and athletics, football, volleyball, throwball, badminton and handball courts, plus indoor games and a campus gymnasium.

Clubs & fests

Technical and cultural activity through the academic year, with inter-college competitions and an annual campus fest.

Residential life

Separate in-campus hostels for boys and girls, with a warden, hygienic kitchens and a varied daily menu.

Sport

Nine disciplines

  • Cricket ground & athletics
  • Football, volleyball & throwball
  • Badminton & handball courts
  • Basketball, kho-kho, kabaddi & netball
  • Table tennis, chess, carrom & gymnasium
Everyday life

Campus amenities

  • Campus-wide Wi-Fi
  • Cafeteria & hygienic canteen
  • On-campus bank & ATM
  • Health & medical centre
  • College transport fleet, parking & photocopy centre
Learning environment

Smart classrooms and mentoring

Teaching takes place in smart classrooms supported by laboratories across the engineering branches, with a structured student-mentoring system that pairs students with faculty through their course.

Students are encouraged to build projects, take part in internal hackathons and complete internships alongside coursework - the students who do see the strongest placement results.
