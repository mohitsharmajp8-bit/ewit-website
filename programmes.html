Academics

Programmes & departments

Eighteen courses spanning engineering, computing and management — undergraduate, postgraduate and research-oriented — delivered on a single 20-acre campus.

Eleven B.E. disciplines

Four-year, eight-semester undergraduate engineering programmes under the VTU framework, with electives designed by the institute under its autonomy.

Computer Science & Engineering

CSE

Algorithms, systems and software engineering, extended with cloud, AI, full-stack and cybersecurity electives.

Information Science & Engineering

ISE

Information systems, databases and software development with industry-aligned electives.

Electronics & Communication

ECE

VLSI, embedded systems and communication engineering with core-industry placements.

Electrical & Electronics

EEE

Power systems, drives and control, with recruiters across energy and utilities.

Mechanical Engineering

ME

Design, thermal and manufacturing engineering with core-sector placements.

Civil Engineering

CV

Structures, geotechnics and construction management, supported by an M.Tech pathway.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

AI&ML

Model building, deep learning and applied ML on GPU-backed laboratory infrastructure.

Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

AI&DS

Analytics, statistics and data engineering for analytics and applied-AI roles.

CSE — IoT, Cybersecurity & Blockchain

CSE-ICB

A specialised CSE track for cybersecurity, distributed systems and fintech roles.

CSE — Artificial Intelligence & ML

CSE-AIML

Computer science core combined with a dedicated machine-learning specialisation.

Allied engineering branches

+

Together with the above, EWIT runs eleven B.E. disciplines in all. Contact the admissions office for the current intake.

Postgraduate programmes

Two-year master's programmes for advanced industry roles and research, plus an MBA with dual-specialisation pathways.

Master of Business Administration

MBA

Core specialisations plus dual-specialisation pathways: Finance & Marketing, Marketing & HR, and HR & Finance.

Master of Computer Applications

MCA

Advanced software development and computing for industry roles.

M.Tech — Computer Science & Engineering

M.Tech CSE

Advanced computing, research methods and specialisation electives.

M.Tech — Digital Electronics

M.Tech DE

Digital system design, VLSI and embedded electronics.

M.Tech — Machine Design

M.Tech MD

Mechanical design, analysis and simulation for core engineering.

M.Tech — Structural Engineering

M.Tech SE

Advanced structural analysis, design and construction materials.

M.Tech — Geotechnical Engineering

M.Tech GTE

Soil mechanics, foundations and ground engineering.
