Laboratories & infrastructure

Where the departments work

Laboratories across ten engineering branches support teaching and research, alongside smart classrooms, departmental reading collections and a structured student-mentoring system.

  • 215+ high-end computing systems with LAN and internet connectivity
  • GPU-backed AI / ML laboratory for model training and evaluation
  • Departmental libraries and a 65,000-volume central library
  • 13 active industry MoUs supporting training, internships and placements
